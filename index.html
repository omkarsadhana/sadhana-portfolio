01Photography

Photography

Photography is the art of capturing moments using a camera. It focuses on composition, lighting, and timing. The goal is to tell a story or preserve a moment in a single image.

02Videography

Videography

Videography is the process of recording moving visuals. It combines camera movement, sound, and storytelling. Used in films, ads, events, and digital content creation.

03Editing

Editing

Editing is refining photos or videos after they are captured. It involves cutting, color correction, and visual enhancement. Editing improves clarity, mood, and overall impact.

04Graphic Design

Graphic Design

Graphic design creates visual content using text and imagery. It focuses on layout, color, typography, and branding. Used in logos, posters, social media, and marketing materials.
